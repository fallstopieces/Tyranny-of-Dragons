The party began the long caravan journey north from Baldur’s Gate with their cover already potentially compromised.

The cultist [[Mirela 'Miri' Valehart]] thought had recognized her at the end of the previous session made direct eye contact with her before walking away. Miri warned the others, but the man did not immediately confront her or openly alert the caravan.

As the first days of travel began, Miri and [[Roxy]] learned more about their young charge, **Orvustia “Essie” Esseren**. Essie revealed that her parents had died violently during a hunting trip when she was young and that she had personally gathered their remains and buried them. Her aunt subsequently raised her and was the person who sent her alone on the caravan journey. Miri and Roxy became deeply suspicious of the aunt’s motives, although there is currently no evidence that Essie was deliberately sent into danger.

During a midday stop, the caravan spotted a magnificent white stag. Many travelers immediately wanted to kill it for its valuable pelt. Miri remembered old stories claiming that the appearance of a white stag foretold a good harvest and that killing one risked angering the gods.

Several party members followed the **stag** and discovered that it was actually an ancient elf prince trapped by a curse. His secret marriage had angered his bride’s father, who cursed him to become a stag whenever he left the borders of the kingdom. So much time has passed that the kingdom itself has fallen into ruins, while the curse has unnaturally prolonged the elf’s life.

He is attempting to reach Waterdeep in hopes that someone there can remove the curse.

The party agreed to help him.

Miri performed an embellished warning ballad before the caravan, invoking angry gods, ruined harvests, and divine punishment for anyone who harmed the stag. With additional persuasion from the party, most of the caravan reluctantly agreed to leave it alive. Essie then successfully hunted an ordinary deer, providing fresh meat and helping reduce the hunters’ interest in the stag.

The cursed elf joined the caravan in stag form.

Later, during heavy rain, one of the wagons overturned and spilled a large quantity of jewelry and valuables onto the road. The owners behaved aggressively toward anyone attempting to help. Miri recognized the same suspected cultist who had previously recognized her among the people hurriedly collecting the valuables.

Shortly afterward, several caravan travelers—including Miri—became violently ill. One gnome died after suffering similar symptoms. [[Roxy]] determined that the illness was some form of poisoning, but the exact source was never established. Many of the victims had been near the overturned wagon, leading the party to suspect that the cult’s treasure or something associated with it might have caused the sickness.

The following morning, the road became covered by rapidly growing, screaming mushrooms.

Although the fungi were not conventionally poisonous, their black spores produced powerful psychological effects. Miri and [[Mori]] were exposed while helping clear the road and became overwhelmed by grief and remorse. Roxy created an herbal treatment to help flush the spores from their systems, but both continued suffering vivid nightmares for several nights.

Mori experienced recurring nightmares involving the mushrooms.

Miri’s dreams were considerably stranger: darkness, thick fog, blood, and a distant voice calling her home. She awoke screaming and clawing toward the nearest fire, insisting that someone was coming for her. Afterward, she began playing an old viol she had not used since before traveling with the party, producing a slow and unsettling melody almost automatically.

The nightmares gradually faded as the spores finally left their systems.

Several days later, the caravan discovered a man buried upright beside the road with only his head exposed and “Oath Breaker” written across his forehead.

The party dug him out.

The man, **Carlin**, explained that he had been engaged to a woman but discovered that her father and brother were bandits who expected him to join their criminal operation. When he refused and ended the engagement, they buried him alive as punishment.

Carlin described himself as more of a spy than a fighter and was also traveling toward Waterdeep. Grateful for being rescued, he offered to accompany and assist the caravan once he recovered.

The session ended with Carlin traveling alongside the group, Miri attempting to drink away several days of mushroom-induced trauma, and the caravan continuing north with both the Cult of the Dragon and an ancient cursed elf hidden among its travelers.
