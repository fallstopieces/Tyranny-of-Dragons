The party entered the keep with the civilians rescued during Session One. The gates were raised or sealed immediately afterward, and raiders surrounded the structure.
Inside were approximately one hundred frightened civilians, including crying children and wounded townspeople. Most of Greenest’s defenders were not professional soldiers, but farmers and ordinary residents wearing whatever equipment they could find. [[Korinn]] talks to the first civilian they rescued and learned her name was [[Linan Swift]]. Korinn then gives her all the stolen goods to give back to the townsfolk. 

Linan then points out [[Governor Nighthill]] to the party and they go and talk to him. [[Mirela 'Miri' Valehart]] is the first to introduce herself and then introduces [[Korinn]] and [[Mori]].  Nighthill thanked them for bringing so many people to safety. He admitted that Greenest had few trained warriors and asked the three adventurers to continue helping. He promised an appropriate reward, though he could not yet specify its value.

He outlined several urgent problems:
1.    Raiders had breached the keep’s sally port.
2.    Townspeople were trapped inside a temple east of the keep.
3.    Raiders appeared to be attempting to burn the town’s mill.
4.    He wanted at least one attacker captured alive for interrogation.

Before the party could decide which external mission to undertake, Escobert the Red, an older red-haired and red-bearded dwarf, arrived to report that enemies had broken through the sally port. The party rushes over to help and manages to kill 3 Kobolds and 1 acolyte while capturing another one. 

The unconscious prisoner was dragged to Governor Nighthill’s private room. Nighthill wanted answers but was uneasy with the party’s preferred interrogation methods.
Miri attempted a gentler approach first, trying to persuade the prisoner to cooperate. He refused.
[[Korinn]] then:
•    Put him in a chokehold.
•    Accidentally rendered him unconscious again.
•    Revived him using a successful Medicine check.
•    Asked whether he wanted to answer questions or “see God again.”
•    Successfully intimidated him into cooperating.
The prisoner demanded that Korinn stop touching him and declared a firm “no-touching zone.”

## Information learned
The prisoner identified himself as a low-ranking initiate of the Cult of the Dragon.
He revealed that:
•    The raiders were primarily in Greenest to steal treasure.
•    They had specifically heard that the town’s families possessed old heirlooms and valuables.
•    The collected treasure was connected to the cult’s larger plans.
•    The cult intended to usher in the Dragon Queen.
•    He was only an initiate and knew relatively little about the higher leadership.
The party understood the Dragon Queen to mean Tiamat. The exact details of how the stolen treasure would assist Tiamat’s return remained unknown.

The party took a short rest before departing.
[[Castellan Escobert the Red]] led them into the keep’s cellar and showed them a locked hatch leading into an old underground passage.
He provided a key and warned them:
•    The tunnel was narrow enough to require single-file travel.
•    It had never been used during a siege.
•    There might be rats.
•    Enemies could be waiting at the far entrance.
•    Rescued civilians should be directed through the tunnel rather than toward the besieged main entrances.
They should remain quiet when emerging.

Once the party got into the tunnel it was immediately obvious that  [[Mirela 'Miri' Valehart]] was terrified of the dark and that [[Korinn]] did not like rats for she sprinted through the tunnel, leaving the party in her dust. This meant Miri and [[Mori]] had to deal with the swarm of rats: 
They attempted several solutions, including:
•    Animal Handling.
•    Using fire.
•    Stabbing the swarm.
•    Dragging one another through it.
•    Screaming.
•    Stomping aggressively.

Escobert heard the commotion, entered the tunnel without a weapon, and simply stomped the remaining rats to death.
At least the tunnel was thereafter safe for civilians.

At the far end, Korinn attempted to open an ancient iron gate and snapped the key inside its rusted lock. The party attempted to dismantle or force the gate. With teamwork and a strong final effort, they tore it open. Unfortunately, the gate produced an enormous metallic shriek, completely ruining Escobert’s advice about remaining quiet. The tunnel opened beside a narrow stream outside the keep. The party emerged exhausted and distracted. Miri and Mori attempted to wash or recover beside the water while Corin caught her breath. The noise from the gate had alerted enemies nearby.

The party followed the stream and used bushes for cover while approaching Greenest’s mill. A strong observation or performance-related check revealed something suspicious:
•    The raiders were not seriously attempting to burn the mill.
•    The small fires were positioned primarily to create the appearance of an active blaze.
•    The mill and its stores were still intact.
This strongly suggested a diversion or trap. After some investigating the party determined this was a diversion and decided to head to the Temple. 

The temple was a large stone building with a steep roof and stained-glass windows.
The party discovered several separate groups of attackers:
•    A patrol of cultists and kobolds circling the building.
•    Guards stationed near the rear entrance.
•    A much larger force at the front.
•    A higher-ranking cult commander supervising a battering ram.
The rear of the temple was concealed by thick black smoke produced by a fire. Creatures seen through more than fifteen feet of the smoke were heavily obscured.
At the front, the attackers repeatedly rammed the main entrance while terrified civilians screamed inside. The party was badly outnumbered. A direct attack would probably have failed. Instead, they split up.

Miri approached the front of the temple openly while carrying stolen trinkets and her instrument.
She claimed that:
•    She was a traveling storyteller.
•    She had come to offer tribute to the Cult of the Dragon.
•    She wanted to hear the cult’s story.
•    She hoped to compose a ballad honoring their queen and mission.
•    She was particularly interested in the commander’s personal story.
The commander [[Eric the Dragonclaw]] was notably better dressed and more important than the initiates the party had encountered earlier. He was also sufficiently attractive that Miri’s interest was not entirely fabricated. Miri rolled an excellent Performance check and received Heroic Inspiration for the idea.

Meanwhile Mori was at the rear of the temple with Korrin:

Mori and Korinn moved toward the rear entrance through the obscuring smoke. Three guards remained near the back door and would not abandon their post merely because of Miri’s performance. Mori used Thaumaturgy to create ominous whispers elsewhere in the smoke.
The guards reacted poorly:
•    One became convinced they were in immediate danger.
•    The other two were hesitant.
•    The panicked guard grabbed his companions and pulled them away to investigate.
Mori remained outside as a lookout while Corin entered the temple. She later used another supernatural whisper to warn Corin when the circling patrol approached.
The rear door was unlocked. Korinn entered and found approximately thirty frightened townspeople.
Many initially recoiled from her because:
•    She was a dragonborn.
•    A blue dragon was actively destroying their town.
•    They could not immediately distinguish between Korinn and the attackers.

A priest approached and apologized for their fear. So Korinn showed him:
•    Her Order of the Gauntlet insignia.
•    Her holy symbol.
•    Her allegiance to Bahamut, whom she described as the “good dragon.”
She explained that Governor Nighthill had sent the party and that the secret tunnel had been cleared so the priest rallied the civilians. When Mori warned that the patrol was approaching, Korinn nearly closed the door and held everyone inside until it passed. The patrol noticed that the rear guards had disappeared but assumed they had been distracted by whatever was happening at the front. Once the coast was clear, Korinn began sending civilians out in groups of two or three.

Miri watched for signs that the evacuation was complete while continuing her performance. After catching sight of Korinn disappearing into the bushes, Miri told the commander that:
•    She needed to freshen up after traveling.
•    She would find him afterward.
•    They could continue their conversation somewhere more private. 
She placed a hand on his chest and strongly implied that there would not be much conversation involved. The commander allowed her to leave. Miri retreated into the bushes and immediately admitted that, although he had been attractive, the entire experience had been terrifying.

The commander survived and may recognize her in the future.

The priest and able-bodied civilians knew the route to the tunnel and helped lead the group. Governor Nighthill was overwhelmed by the number of people they had rescued and thanked the party repeatedly. Before the conversation could continue, the keep shook violently.

The blue dragon roared overhead.
Escobert arrived with the news:
The dragon was attacking the keep.
The session ended there.
