The party helped Greenest’s surviving defenders drive away the adult blue dragon attacking the keep. During the battle, [[Korinn]] heroically, or stupidly, leapt from the battlements onto the dragon, was carried away, fell approximately thirty feet, and lost her left leg below the knee. A mysterious healer later regenerated the limb, which grew back in a way no one will soon forget.

Before the exhausted party could rest, the blue half-dragon champion [[Langdedrosa Cyanwrath]] appeared outside the keep with civilian hostages and demanded that Greenest send out its best warrior. A renewed, and pissed off, Korinn accepted immediately and without even touching him, Korinn was swiftly defeated, but her participation secured the hostages’ release.

The night was finally over, the dragon flew off and the raiders all disappeared into the night. At long last the party got to have a long rest.

After finally resting, the party was commissioned by Governor Nighthill to follow the retreating raiders, locate their camp, determine their numbers and purpose, and recover information about the Cult of the Dragon. They were also asked to search for the missing monk [[Leosin Erlanthar]] by his apprentice [[Nesim Waladra]]. 

During their preparations, they encountered and recruited the hungover sorceress [[Ilyona]]. The first stop they made was to The Greenest Green where they met shop owner [[Bob of Greenest Green]]. He was noticeably high, but not really willing to share. The party wasn't able to buy much since most raided but they were able to get some goods then set out on their way. 

The party followed the trail and found some straggelers from the rear guard. At this point the party causes a rockslide then interogates those still alive. Once they are satisfied with the information, Korrin kills them and steals their robes as a disguise. The party found out that the actual rear guard isn't too far ahead so they come up with a plan:
The party developed a disguise:
•    Miri wore a bloodstained cultist robe.
•    She used the false name Jezebel.
•    Korinn was covered in blood and loosely bound as a prisoner.
•    Korinn was gagged.
•    Miri carried the rope and claimed to have captured her.
•    The other party members followed through the surrounding terrain.
Miri claimed:
•    She had only been with the cult for about three weeks.
•    Her group had been killed in a rockslide.
•    She had captured the Dragonborn afterward.
•    She wanted to take the prisoner back to camp.
•    She was exhausted, filthy, hungry, and desperately needed a bath.

The leader eventually ordered three cultists to escort Miri and Korinn to the main camp.

One additional cultist was sent back to investigate the stragglers.

Ilyona moved through the surrounding brush with exceptional stealth, rolling a natural twenty. Mori was nearly discovered while creeping forward. She avoided capture by transforming into a rat. The two followed Miri, Korinn, and their escorts toward the camp.

Inside the camp, they learned that the Cult of the Dragon was gathering an enormous treasure hoard in preparation for the return of Tiamat. They discovered that several prisoners had been psychologically broken into obedient servants and located Leosin among a separate group of captives. The party realizes later on that the only girl in this camp was the person who seemed to be in charge. This person was [[Frulam Mondath]], who gets mistaken as a man all the time.

The escorts delivered Miri and Korinn to the camp’s large command tent. Mondath was sharpening a weapon when they entered.

Rather than continuing the increasingly unstable “Jezebel” story, Miri told Mondath a version of the truth.
She explained that:
•    She was a traveling bard.
•    Korinn  was her bodyguard.
•    Stories involving dragons were irresistible to her.
•    She wanted to learn the cult’s history.
•    She hoped to immortalize Mondath and the cult in song.
•    Her performances could potentially earn money for Tiamat’s hoard.
Mondath immediately recognized that Miri was not truly a cultist. He was nevertheless impressed—or at least entertained—that she had successfully infiltrated the camp. He also recognized the danger in allowing her to remain free.

## Mondath’s decision

Mondath ordered that:
•    Their weapons be confiscated.
•    They remain in camp temporarily.
•    They obey all orders.
•    They be watched carefully.
•    Any suspicious behavior be reported directly to him.
•    They work as guards over a group of prisoners.
He warned that one mistake would result in their deaths. Miri was allowed to retain her writing materials because she convincingly argued that she could not record his story without them.

Miri and Korinn were assigned to watch a tent filled with captives. The prisoners inside were alive but psychologically destroyed.
They:
•    Followed orders automatically.
•    Praised Tiamat mechanically.
•    Showed little independent thought.
•    Appeared incapable of attempting escape.
•    Had been reduced to obedient laborers.
Miri and Korinn concluded that their “guard duty” was merely intended to keep them occupied. The prisoners were too thoroughly broken to require supervision. The discovery also demonstrated what could eventually happen to Leosin if he remained a captive.

# Ilyona’s Investigation
Ilyona walked openly through the camp as though she belonged there. Because the raiders were drunk and celebrating, few people challenged her. She joined a group of intoxicated cultists and questioned them.
## Information learned

The cultists confirmed that:
•    They had captured numerous prisoners.
•    The more dangerous or useful prisoners were kept elsewhere in camp.
•    The prisoners near the entrance were closely guarded.
•    The treasure stolen from Greenest was intended for Tiamat.
•    The cult was building a hoard worthy of the Dragon Queen before her return.
•    They believed Tiamat would soon return to the world.
•    Lower-ranking cultists did not know the full ritual or plan.
•    They did not know where the cult would raid next.
•    They simply followed instructions from higher-ranking leaders.
Ilyona also noticed that the camp contained remarkably few women and that she therefore stood out more than expected.


Mori transformed into a cave badger and dug an underground escape route. Korinn extracted Leosin—against his wishes, because he had allowed himself to be captured deliberately—and the party escaped through the tunnel while alarms rang throughout the camp. 

After a quick retreat the party makes it back to [[Greenest]] without anymore issues and they deliver their intel to [[Governor Nighthill of Greenest]] and then reach level 3.
