After recovering from the events at the dragon hatchery, the party left Greenest and traveled north toward Elturel, where Leosin Erlanthar had instructed them to find his ally [[Ontharr Frume]]

Their six-day journey was uneventful.

In Elturel, the party located Ontharr at the exceptionally rowdy tavern **A Pair of Black Antlers**. Before discussing the Cult of the Dragon, Ontharr insisted that they relax, drink, fight, perform, and generally demonstrate what sort of people they were.

[[Mirela 'Miri' Valehart]] performed the memorial song she had written for Corin with Roxy’s permission. [[Calib]] participated in arm wrestling and drank heavily. [[Roxy]] conducted an actual wedding ceremony between two willing tavern patrons. [[Leosin]] also formally approached Miri about affiliating with the Harpers, giving her a Harper pin and explaining the faction’s ideals.

Later that evening, Ontharr and Leosin privately briefed the party. They explained that multiple organizations—including the Harpers and Order of the Gauntlet—were beginning to cooperate against the Cult of the Dragon.

The cult’s stolen treasure was moving north, but no one yet knew its ultimate purpose or destination.

The party’s new mission was to:

•    Reach Baldur’s Gate before the cult.

•    Wait for the cult’s wagons to arrive.

•    Join the same northbound merchant caravan as hired guards.

•    Blend into the ordinary caravan population.

•    Follow the cult’s wagons without revealing themselves.

•    Determine where the treasure was ultimately being taken.

Ontharr provided 50 gp to each party member and arranged river transportation from Elturel.

The party left their horses safely behind in Elturel and traveled by boat down the River Chionthar to Baldur’s Gate.

There they contacted one of Leosin and Ontharr’s allies, an equipment merchant in the Blackgate district. The party spent approximately twelve days preparing, shopping, crafting memorial items, and watching for the cult.

When the cult finally arrived, they observed known or suspected cult members purchasing new wagons and supplies. The sharper-eyed members of the group also caught a fleeting glimpse of a half-dragon traveling inside a covered litter or palanquin, although the figure’s identity was not explicitly confirmed.

The party then successfully obtained employment with the larger northbound caravan:

•    Miri and Roxy were assigned to protect a young traveler named Orvustia Esseren.

•    Calib and Ilyona were assigned to guard Beyd Sechepol, a half-elf merchant selling ale and beer.

•    Mori was hired in a caravan sergeant role.

The caravan journey is expected to last approximately forty days.

Just before the session ended, Miri noticed someone she believed she recognized from the raiders’ camp.

The man looked directly at her.

He appeared to recognize her as well.

Then he quickly looked away and walked off.
