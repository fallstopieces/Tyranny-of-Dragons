
# How the Party Met

The three originally met in a tavern. Miri noticed Corin and Mori from where she was performing and began teasing and flirting with them through her song. Their reactions entertained her, so she continued singling them out. The group brainstormed an altercation involving a man aggressively attempting to flirt with [[Korinn]]:

•    Korinn was too naïve to respond to his interest normally.

•    The man shoved Mori aside while attempting to reach Korinn.

•    [[Mori]] was willing to intervene once he physically inconvenienced her.

•    Korinn eventually grabbed the man by the back of his head, slammed his face into the bar, and ordered him onto the floor.

•    Mori robbed him.

•    Korinn casually stood on one of his hands.

•    [[Mirela 'Miri' Valehart]] was delighted by the entire situation.

Miri effectively attached herself to Korinn and Mori afterward.

They chose to continue traveling together because:

•    Korinn had orders to report toward Greenest.

•    Miri wanted stories involving dragons and cults.

•    Mori was already moving on and liked the financial possibilities.

•    Traveling as a group of women appeared safer.

•    Miri could attract and earn money from troublesome men, Korinn could incapacitate them, and Mori could rob them afterward.

The table summarized their emerging philosophy as supporting both “women’s rights and women’s wrongs.”

After several days traveling through the rolling grasslands of the Greenfields, the party crested a hill near sundown and saw Greenest several miles away. Instead of the peaceful settlement they expected, they found: Greenest in flames, they could hear screaming coming from the town below and most terrifying was the blue dragon circling around. But the party put on a brave face and continued onward. 

First thing they encountered was a family, an injured adult man, three children, and a woman trying to defend her family against 7 Kobolds. The party stepped in to protect the family and healed the husband, fully expecting him to pick up arms and help defend his family. While the party continued towards the keep they encountered a few more enemies and rescured more civilians, gathering quite a group. The party made it to the keep just in time, because as soon as they came through defenders boarded up the entrances. Tired and ready for rest the party looked for who was in charge and were led to [[Governor Nighthill of Greenest]].
