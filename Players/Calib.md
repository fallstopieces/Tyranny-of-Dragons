Brother to [[Korinn]] and [[Roxanya]]

He is the biggest of his siblings, a tall black dragonborn who was physically gifted. At the age of 5 he was already helping the local farmers till there fields and carry lumber for the lumberjacks. Then when dad let him help him in the forge he excelled greatly in terms of strength. And with his competition with Korinn he only grew faster. By the time he was 10 he was already able to fix most weapons and armor without help and able to use just about any weapon with relative ease. He grew so fast and so large that his black scales would crack and when they healed the scares were a faint gold color from where they cracked but for some reason all these other scares didn’t heal that way. When the village was attacked, he did all he could to protect his family but was not successful he was Korrin get picked up by a knight and his younger brother was literally ripped from his grasp when he was knocked out when he came to, he was in the village and he tried to find his parents but all he could find was their charred bodies. After he buried them, he swore that he would find his siblings and reunite the family and he set off after about 3 years of traveling on his own he ran into some dwarves in the mountain who took him in and healed him from his travels. He then traded his services of strength for them to train him and they taught him how to use weapons the correct way and taught him rune craft. When he then set out he was trying to hunt down any information on his siblings that he could find and as time dragged on and the years passed, he grew less and less hopeful and when he started to give up home the gold scares he had started to tarnish and darken. He came across some news about his younger sister and on his way to find her he got into a bit of a scuffle and when he came to he had found is other sister Roxanya. And the two of them have set out together to find korrin
