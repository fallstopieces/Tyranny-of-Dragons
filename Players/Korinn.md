Sister to [[Calib]] and [[Roxanya]]

she was borne to a Black Dragonborn ( her father) named Darious and a Red Dragonborn (her mother) named Clivinia she has 3 other siblings, the first to hatch ( the oldest) was her brother Calib a black dragonborn like their father the second one to hatch was her older sister a blue dragonborn named Roxanya ( Roxy for short) then the third to hatch was Rhogar a Brass dragonborn with scales that looked almost black then there was Korrin a Red Dragonborn with piercing blue eyes. She was always in some kind of competition with Torinn and it all started with how fast they grew then who got their breath weapon first Torinn beat her on that one, but everyone was shocked when they saw Korrins blue flames for the first time. Her dad would work at the local town as a black smith with the help of her mother who was also a tanner and seamstress they made an honest living. They were a happy family, but that only lasted for 10 years when mom and dad came running back to the house on the outskirts of town battered and bruised, the town was under attack. All Korrin remembers from that day is that people, if black and purple cloaks took her family away from her and if it wasn’t for the brave knight that saved whisked her away fleeing the battlefield she would’ve been taken too.

                The knight brought her to the Order of The Gauntlet, and she started training every day and every night. She gave herself no time for the small pleasures like going to town with the other recruits or getting drunk at mealtime all that mattered was that she got stronger, strong enough to protect her new family and save her old one. She learned of Bahamut and devoted herself to his ancient power swore to drive away all the evils of the world. It wasn’t till she was 13 when she had her first encounter with evil since the incident back home, a man tried to take advantage of her one day when she was out collecting groceries for the meal that night. The man took her to a warehouse where a group of men were there taking advantage of about 20 other females of all different ages and races it was at that moment that she realized that all men contain evil inside of them and she will be the one to help them see the light and errors of their ways. Needless to say with her training she had been receiving these last years and the sudden divine power that was pulsing threw her body she led all these women to safety and took in the whole group of evil men. For that day on all she did was train so that she can rid the world of evil ( men ). ( she does understand that not all men are evil but 95% of them are).
