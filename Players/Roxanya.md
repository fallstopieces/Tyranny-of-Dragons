Sister to [[Calib]] and [[Korinn]]

She is tall, slender and elegant and carries herself with a sense of grace. After her village was attacked, she ran into a group of clerics on their way to a new monastery. There they patched her up and adapted her into the monastery and a fledgling cleric. Years have passed and she has led a fairly simple life healing the sick and wounded. She has learned so much about all the different gods and connected most with Bahamut and (…). It was years of hard work, and she was finally healing from her trauma of losing her family. But one day a wonderer came in who was battered and bruised clearly from a fight that he got into. And when she went to heal him and tended to his wounds she froze. It was her brother Calib. He was alive and he was changed. He was always the biggest and strongest in the family and had this intimidating look, but he was always kind and would help others no matter how big he was or scary he was truly just a soft giant. That softness has faded into a feeling of danger. But she couldn’t help but realize it was from the pain of losing his family, they found each other. And when he awoke the reunion was heartwarming. He filled her in on what happened to him and said that he had been looking for their family this whole time and know he is one step closer. Once he was fully healed, she made up her mind to leave the monastery to join her brother on his journey to find and reunite the family.
